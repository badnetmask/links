Privacy Overview

This is a personal links page. It is intentionally minimal and collects as little as possible.

Analytics

This site uses Umami, a self-hosted, cookie-free analytics tool, to see basic aggregate visit counts. It does not fingerprint you, build a cross-site profile, or share data with anyone.

This site makes no attempt to evade ad blockers or tracker blockers. If your browser or network blocks it, your visit simply isn't counted, and that choice is respected.

Third-party content

Images, fonts, icons, and styles are served directly from this domain. The only other host contacted is the analytics service above, which is self-hosted on infrastructure I also control.

Hosting

This site is self-hosted on a Microsoft Azure virtual machine in the East US 2 region, which I administer myself. Azure is the underlying infrastructure provider, and its network and platform layers may log or process connection metadata (such as your IP address) on their own. I don't control and have no visibility into what happens at that level.

Changes

This page and its practices may change at any time, without notice.
